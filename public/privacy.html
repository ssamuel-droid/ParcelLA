Privacy Policy

This policy explains how ParcelLA collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you use the ParcelLA website, account, underwriting, records, export, and payment services.

Your privacy

ParcelLA uses account and activity information to provide and secure the service. It does not sell personal information or use it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

1. Scope and operator

ParcelLA operates the ParcelLA service. This policy applies to information handled through the ParcelLA website, applications, account system, communications, underwriting tools, exports, and billing features. It does not govern third-party websites, government record systems, or linked document repositories.

2. Information collected

Account information. ParcelLA may collect your name, email address, authentication identifiers, account status, saved properties, notes, model settings, alert preferences, and records of your acceptance of the Terms.

Payment information. Stripe processes card and bank payments. ParcelLA receives transaction, subscription, customer, payment-status, and limited billing information needed to provide access and maintain records, but does not receive or store complete card or bank-account numbers.

Usage and technical information. ParcelLA may collect searches, property views, export and feature usage, browser and device information, IP address, timestamps, security events, error logs, and similar operational data.

Communications. ParcelLA collects information you provide in support, legal, privacy, billing, or other communications.

Property and public-record information. ParcelLA processes property addresses, APNs, ownership evidence, applicant and representative names, permits, planning cases, recorded transactions, mortgages, sales, plans, and other government or commercial property data. This information may concern people who are not ParcelLA account holders and may be incomplete or inaccurate.

3. How information is used

ParcelLA uses information to create and authenticate accounts; provide searches, underwriting, source documents, exports, sharing, saved properties, and alerts; process purchases and subscriptions; maintain payment entitlements; communicate about the service; prevent fraud, scraping, abuse, and security incidents; troubleshoot and improve performance; comply with law; enforce the Terms; and protect ParcelLA, users, and third parties.

4. How information is disclosed

Information may be disclosed to service providers that support hosting, databases, authentication, payments, email, mapping, analytics, security, and property-data functions, including Supabase, Stripe, Railway, Vercel, Resend, mapping providers, government-data systems, and property-data providers. These providers handle information for their respective services and may apply their own terms and privacy notices.

Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with law or legal process, protect rights and safety, investigate misuse, enforce agreements, collect amounts owed, or complete a financing, reorganization, merger, asset sale, or similar business transaction.

ParcelLA does not sell personal information and does not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. ParcelLA does not disclose private account notes or saved-property activity to other users unless you direct a sharing feature to do so.

5. Cookies and local storage

ParcelLA and its authentication and payment providers use browser storage, cookies, and similar technologies to maintain sessions, remember settings, continue checkout, record required acknowledgments, prevent misuse, and operate the service. Blocking these technologies may prevent login, payment, or other features from working. ParcelLA does not currently use third-party advertising cookies.

6. Retention

ParcelLA retains information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain account and transaction records, document legal consent, resolve disputes, protect security, enforce agreements, satisfy tax and legal obligations, and maintain appropriate backups. Retention periods vary by data type and purpose. Public property records may remain available from their original source even after ParcelLA removes a cached copy.

7. Security

ParcelLA uses administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards intended to protect information, including authenticated access controls and restricted server-side data access. No system, transmission, or storage method is completely secure, and ParcelLA cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices and privacy requests

You may update available account settings, cancel a subscription through the billing controls, and unsubscribe from nonessential marketing communications using the provided link or by contacting ParcelLA. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with your ParcelLA account. ParcelLA may need to verify your identity and may retain information when permitted or required by law.

California residents may have additional rights under applicable law, including rights to know, correct, delete, or obtain information about certain data practices and to exercise rights without unlawful discrimination. Because ParcelLA does not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, it does not offer a sale/sharing opt-out. Authorized-agent requests must include authority sufficient to verify the request.

9. Browser privacy signals

ParcelLA does not use personal information for targeted advertising across unaffiliated services. Because there is no universally accepted standard for Do Not Track signals, ParcelLA does not separately respond to them. ParcelLA will honor legally required browser-based opt-out signals if its practices become subject to such a requirement.

10. Children

ParcelLA is intended for adults and business users and is not directed to children under 18. ParcelLA does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact ParcelLA if you believe a child has provided account information.

11. Changes

ParcelLA may update this policy as the service, providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version. Material changes may be presented through the service or by email when appropriate.

12. Contact

Send privacy requests, questions, or complaints to ssamuel@goodhealthcorp.com. Include enough information to identify your ParcelLA account and describe the request, but do not email passwords, complete payment credentials, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Property records are not title reports
